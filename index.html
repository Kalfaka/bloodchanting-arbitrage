How Bloodchanting Arbitrage Works

1

Buy Items from Trade Post

Purchase items using bags (1 bag = 100m GP). Look for items with LOW bags/token or bags/shard conversion rates.

2

Convert to Shop Currency

Turn items into Blood Shard or Token shops to receive currency. Craft Bloodchanting Stones (250 shards + 500 tokens + 10 diamonds).

3

Sell for Profit

Sell Bloodchanting Stones on trade post. If craft cost < market price = profit! Calculator shows optimal strategy.

Key Insight: Lower bags/token or bags/shard = cheaper materials = higher profit margins! Positive ROI means better than average deal.
